Landforms

A landform is a shaped place on a terrain: a volcano, an impact crater, a flat construction pad, a gulley, a raised cover for a tunnel. Each is a piece, placed like a lamp is, and it shapes the ground under it. A volcano also carries its own province (the glow of its caldera and flanks) and its own vent: an effect the terrain places on the crater, as busy as the volcano's activity says. Move the volcano and the cone, the glow and the smoke move together.

{
  "id": "fuji",
  "at": [400, 0, -900],
  "features": { "volcano": { "radius": 420, "height": 260, "activity": 0.5 } }
}

The piece's x and z are the landform's centre; its height is not used (a landform is a change to the ground, which has its own height). The rest is tosijs-3d's own schema for the factory (landformSchemas, tosijs-3d#119, ours). A field such as a volcano's craterRadius that upstream works out from another (radius × 0.22) is left out of the document until an author sets it, so it keeps following its source; the panel shows the number it works out to.

A landform applies to every terrain in the ensemble, or only to the one whose piece id its terrain names. The terrain composes all of them (in document order, each seeing the ground the previous one left), merges their provinces by max, gathers their vents, and regenerates.
